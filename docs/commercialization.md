# Space Commercialization Plan

Last updated: 2026-07-12

## Positioning

Space should be positioned as an Arc-like workspace for Chrome:

> One thing. One Space.

The strongest promise is not generic vertical tabs. It is a focused unit of
work that keeps related pages together.

Use `Arc-like` deliberately in store discovery copy so people looking for an
Arc workflow in Chrome can recognize the product quickly.

## Target Users

- Chrome power users with many tabs and recurring work contexts.
- Users moving from workspace-oriented browsers back to Chrome.
- Developers, product managers, researchers, consultants, founders, and
  students who switch between projects throughout the day.
- People managing projects or decisions across many web sources.
- BYOK AI users who prefer local-first context over hosted productivity suites.

## Recommended Model

Start with a free Chrome Web Store release and validate retention before adding
a paid tier.

Free release:

- Spaces, folders, tab groups, and dormant saved tabs.
- Portable local backup and restore.
- Panel shortcuts.
- Local Auto Group fallback.
- BYOK Ask Space grounded in the active page.

Future Pro tier:

- Cross-device sync.
- Space templates.
- Advanced AI synthesis, planning, and workspace cleanup.
- Shareable export packs for research sessions.
- Team-shared Space templates and onboarding packs.

## Pricing Hypotheses

- Individual monthly: USD 3 to 5.
- Individual early lifetime: USD 19 to 39.
- Team monthly: USD 5 to 8 per seat.

Because Chrome Web Store Payments is deprecated, paid features should be sold
through an external checkout and unlocked with an account or license token.
The first store release should not require sign-in.

## Launch Sequence

1. Publish an unlisted beta build and invite a small group of tab-heavy Chrome
   users.
2. Watch support issues, uninstall reasons, reviews, and qualitative feedback.
3. Publish a public free release once onboarding, privacy copy, screenshots,
   and reviewer notes are stable.
4. Add a product landing page with privacy policy, feedback form, release notes,
   and paid waitlist.
5. Build Pro only after there is evidence that users return to Spaces across
   multiple days.

## Product Principles

- Keep the core local-first and usable without AI.
- Request optional host permissions at the moment of need, not during install.
- Keep AI scoped to an explicit user action and the selected page.
- Charge for durable value such as sync, templates, and team reuse.
- Avoid ads and affiliate links. Trust is a more important asset for this
  category than short-term revenue.
